How to Play Labyrinth .VS

Race through a maze full of walls your opponent has hidden. Whoever reaches the goal first wins. A battle of memory and mind games.

Play Labyrinth .VS

What kind of game?

Each player builds a 6×6 maze. A maze has a “Start (S)”, a “Goal (G)” and a number of “walls”. Your opponent can only see where S and G are; the walls are secret.

Take turns entering your opponent’s maze and find the way while bumping into invisible walls. The first player to reach the goal of the opponent’s maze wins.

Up to 8 people can join one room. Anyone not playing can watch as a spectator.

Switch the language on the first screen (日本語 / English / 한국어). Each player sees the game in their own language, so friends using different languages can play in the same room.

Getting started

  1. Enter your name and tap “Create a room”.
  2. Tell your friends the 4-digit room ID on the screen.
  3. Friends enter the ID and tap “Join”.
  4. The person who created the room (the host) chooses the format and number of walls, then taps “Start game”.

Building your maze

  • Choose the “S” or “G” button and tap a square to place the start and the goal.
  • Choose “Wall” and tap between two squares to place a wall. Tap again to remove it.
  • The host chooses the number of walls, from 10 to 20. Walls can’t go on the outer edge.
  • Always leave at least one path from the start to the goal (the game tells you if there isn’t one).
  • When you’re done, tap “Ready”.

Battle rules

  • Who goes first is decided at random.
  • On your turn, tap the next square to move one square at a time.
  • You can keep moving as many squares as you like until you hit a wall. When you hit one, it’s your opponent’s turn.
  • Walls you hit turn red, so you can remember them. Your next turn continues from where you stopped.
  • Going back the way you came is fine.

Winning and losing

  • If the second player reaches the goal first, the second player wins.
  • If the first player reaches the goal first, it’s “Sudden Death”: the second player gets one last turn. If they reach the goal without hitting a single wall, it’s a draw; if not, the first player wins.
  • Both players get the same number of turns, so it’s fair.

Doubles

Split into two teams, A1 & A2 and B1 & B2. The two teammates build one maze together. One places the start and the other the goal, and they take turns placing walls one at a time. In battle, teammates take turns moving the piece, like A1 → B1 → A2 → B2.

Multi

  • One maze maker builds a maze, and everyone else (the challengers) tries it.
  • Challengers move in order. You can move up to 2 squares per turn.
  • Walls anyone hits become visible to everyone, so other players’ mistakes are clues too.
  • When everyone has moved once, that’s one round. If nobody reaches the goal within the round limit the host sets (15–20 rounds), the maze maker wins.

Coin Battle (option)

When the host turns it on, everyone starts with 5 coins. Win and you get +2; lose and you get −2. Spectators can bet 1 coin on who will win. Turn on “Bonus Wall” too, and spectators can pick one wall they think a player will hit — if they’re right, they get a bonus.

Events

With 4 or more people, you can play a Tournament, a League (Single or Doubles), or an Official match (choose the format for each match). Tap “Standings” at the top left to check the ranking at any time.

vs. Computer

There are three levels: “Easy”, “Normal” and “Hard”. “Normal” remembers walls it hits, and “Hard” also looks for the shortest way to the goal. Perfect for learning the rules.

Tips for winning

💡 Don’t pile all your walls around the goal. Spread them along the detours and your opponent is more likely to get lost.
💡 Use the red walls to read your opponent’s maze: “If there’s a wall here, the path is probably over there.” That’s where the game is won.
💡 When you go second, remember a safe route so you can reach the goal in Sudden Death without hitting a wall.
