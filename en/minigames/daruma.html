Red Light, Green Light!

Party-Arcade mini-gameJudgment

The classic “Red Light, Green Light” (“Daruma-san ga koronda” in Japan), played by driving like a car with “DASH” and “BRAKE”. If you’re moving when the watcher turns around, you’re out!

Play Party-Arcade

What kind of game?

There are two buttons. While you hold “DASH” on the right, you speed up and reach top speed (3.5m/s, about 13km/h) in about 1.2 seconds. Even when you let go, you don’t stop right away: you slow down gradually and, from top speed, stop after moving about 3m.

To stop, use “BRAKE” on the left. Even from top speed, you stop in about 0.3 seconds and about 0.5m. If you press both, the brake wins. The watcher is 25m away. Touch the watcher to reach the goal.

How to play

  1. While the watcher is saying the call, DASH forward.
  2. Before the watcher turns around, stop with BRAKE.
  3. When the watcher faces away again, DASH!
  4. Touch the watcher to reach the goal.

Results and ranking

Players who touch the watcher always rank above those who don’t. Among goal players, a faster time ranks higher. Players who don’t reach the goal (caught or out of time) rank higher the shorter the remaining “m to go”. If you don’t move at all, you get 0 points.

The watcher’s rules

  • The watcher says the call 3 or 4 times, the same way for everyone.
  • There are 4 styles of saying it: normal, speeding up, fast at the start, and a long pause before the end.
  • You’re safe for 0.2 seconds after the watcher turns around. After that, any movement means you’re out.
  • Even after 15 seconds, you still have a last chance until the watcher finishes the last call.
  • In English the call is “Red light, green light!”, in Korean “무궁화 꽃이 피었습니다”, in Japanese “だるまさんがころんだ”. The timing is the same in every language.

Tips to get better

💡 At top speed you need to brake a little before the watcher turns around. Cut it too close and you won’t stop in time.
💡 Learn the watcher’s styles. With the “long pause” style, the last word takes a long time to come.
💡 The speed bar and km/h display tell you how quickly you can stop right now.
💡 The last call crosses the 15-second mark. Don’t give up when time is up!

Behind the game

🛠 It used to be a simple “hold the button to move” game; now you drive with an accelerator and a brake. The key is that you can’t stop instantly when you let go.
🛠 The watcher’s calls and timing are the same for everyone, so the winner is whoever dares to push closest to the limit.

About “Daruma-san ga koronda”

“Daruma-san ga koronda” is a kind of tag game played in Japan for a long time — the Japanese version of “Red Light, Green Light”. You can only move while the watcher is facing away, and you must freeze when they turn around. This game keeps that rule as it is.

Practicing alone

From “Practice mode” on the Party-Arcade home screen, you can practice just this game alone without entering a room (records are not saved). Trying the rules before playing with friends is a good idea.
