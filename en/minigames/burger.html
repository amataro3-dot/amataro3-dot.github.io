Build a Muddy’s Burger!

Party-Arcade mini-gameAction

Catch the falling ingredients on your plate and stack a hamburger. The neater and more centered, the higher the score. Lean too much and the tower collapses.

Play Party-Arcade

What kind of game?

Ingredients fall one at a time. It starts with the bottom bun, then lettuce, patty, tomato, onion and cheese fall in a different order each time. Before the top bun comes ketchup! Put the top bun on last to complete it. The order of ingredients is the same for everyone.

Move your finger left and right “below the ground line” to move the plate. The plate follows your finger with a slight delay, so moving suddenly makes the tower wobble. Ingredients fall faster and faster.

How to play

  1. Move the plate to where the ingredient is falling.
  2. Catch it on the center of the top ingredient.
  3. For ketchup, follow the wiggly bottle and catch it on the top ingredient.
  4. Put the top bun on to complete it!

Results and ranking

The result is a score; higher is better. On the result screen, the burger you made is shown just as it is, crooked and all.

Scoring

WhatPoints

Ketchup% landed on the ingredient = points (up to +100)
Complete (the top bun is on at the end)+100
Dropped ingredients (including ones that fell when it collapsed)0

The maximum is 1000 points. For equal scores, the player whose stack was less off-center ranks higher. 0 points gives 0 pts.

How each ingredient behaves

IngredientBehavior

TomatoSlippery (falls off with a small offset)
CheeseSticky (stays on even when off-center, hard to topple)
OnionNarrow

Tips to get better

💡 Moving your finger suddenly makes the tower wobble. Move slowly and smoothly.
💡 If the tower is leaning to one side, place the next ingredient slightly toward the other side to rebalance it.
💡 Tomato is slippery, so aim especially for the center. Cheese stays on easily even when off-center.
💡 For ketchup, stay one step ahead of the bottle’s movement. It’s worth 100 points, so keep focused to the end!

Behind the game

🛠 Each ingredient has a different weight, size and slipperiness. The same move lands differently depending on the ingredient — that’s what makes the game fun.
🛠 Even after the game ends, falling ingredients keep falling to the bottom of the screen. This keeps the last falling ingredient from looking like it stopped on top of the tower.

About the name

The burger, hamburg steak, curry and Jack games are based on the menu of the creator’s own restaurant. Read the story in the dev diary.

Practicing alone

From “Practice mode” on the Party-Arcade home screen, you can practice just this game alone without entering a room (records are not saved). Trying the rules before playing with friends is a good idea.
