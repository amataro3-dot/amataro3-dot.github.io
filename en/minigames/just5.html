Stop at X Seconds!

Party-Arcade mini-gameSense

Hit “STOP” at exactly the target time. The numbers disappear partway, so all you can rely on is your inner clock.

Play Party-Arcade

What kind of game?

The target is between 5 and 9 seconds in 0.5-second steps (5, 5.5, 6 … 9 seconds — 9 options), and everyone in the room gets the same target.

When the game starts, the numbers on screen start counting, but after 2 seconds they turn into “?.??” and you can’t see them anymore. Keep counting in your head and hit STOP when you think the time is exactly right. When you press it, the real time is shown.

How to play

  1. Check the target time during the countdown.
  2. After the start, use the first 2 seconds to get a feel for the speed of the numbers.
  3. When the numbers disappear, keep counting in your head.
  4. Hit STOP when you think it’s exactly right!

Results and ranking

The result is shown as how far off you were, like “Off by 0.120 sec (target 7.5 sec)”. The smaller, the higher you rank. If you don’t press within 5 seconds after the target time, you’re out of time and get 0 points.

Tips to get better

💡 The first 2 seconds, while the numbers are visible, are your chance to tune your counting rhythm. Silently match “one, two” to the numbers.
💡 For half-second targets like “7.5 sec”, count each second in two beats (“one-and”), which makes the half second easier to count.
💡 When you’re nervous, you tend to count faster. Try to count a little slower and you’ll get closer.

Behind the game

🛠 At first this was “5 Seconds Just”, where the target was always 5 seconds. Now the target changes every time.
🛠 Everyone in the room gets the same target, so it’s clear who is the most accurate.

Practicing alone

From “Practice mode” on the Party-Arcade home screen, you can practice just this game alone without entering a room (records are not saved). Trying the rules before playing with friends is a good idea.
