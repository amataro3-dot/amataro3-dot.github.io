Dodge It!

Party-Arcade mini-gameAction

Move left and right to keep dodging all kinds of things falling from above. Last 15 seconds to clear. Get hit even once and it’s over.

Play Party-Arcade

What kind of game?

Move your finger left and right in the area “below the ground line” to move your character. Your finger doesn’t cover the character, so you can see what’s falling.

The falling objects are the same for everyone, and they get more numerous and faster as time goes on. It’s hardest around 10 seconds.

How to play

  1. Put your finger below the ground line.
  2. Watch the falling objects and move left and right to dodge.
  3. Survive 15 seconds to clear.

Results and ranking

The result is how many seconds you survived; longer is better.

Tips to get better

💡 Staying near the middle of the screen makes it easy to escape either way.
💡 Watch out when something glows red — a pillar is about to land there.
💡 A splitting rock spreads left and right after it splits. Escape a little to the side, not straight below.
💡 Your character has a top speed. Start moving early instead of waiting until the last moment.

Behind the game

🛠 So that there is never a moment that can’t be dodged, every possible movement is checked when the game is made. If an undodgeable moment is found, fewer objects are dropped before it. So every round always has a way to clear it.

Practicing alone

From “Practice mode” on the Party-Arcade home screen, you can practice just this game alone without entering a room (records are not saved). Trying the rules before playing with friends is a good idea.
