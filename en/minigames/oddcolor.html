Spot the Odd Color!

Party-Arcade mini-gameObservation

Among squares that look the same, find the one with a slightly different color. Compete on how many you can find in 15 seconds.

Play Party-Arcade

What kind of game?

It starts with 3×3 squares and an easy color difference (15%). With each correct answer, the difference shrinks by 20% (down to 2%) and the grid grows up to 7×7.

Tap a wrong square and you get a 1-second pause, during which you can’t do anything.

How to play

  1. Tap the square you think has a different color.
  2. If you’re right, the next question appears right away.
  3. Keep solving until 15 seconds are up.

Results and ranking

The result is the number of correct answers; more is better. If you get none right, you get 0 points.

Tips to get better

💡 Instead of staring at one square, look at the whole grid loosely — the odd color tends to pop out.
💡 Later on, the color difference is tiny. Focus on brightness rather than the color itself.
💡 Random taps cost a 1-second pause. Tapping only once you’ve found it is faster in the end.
💡 Turning up your screen brightness makes small differences easier to see.

Behind the game

🛠 The questions keep getting harder, so solving the first few quickly is key. A late question can take many times longer than an early one.

Practicing alone

From “Practice mode” on the Party-Arcade home screen, you can practice just this game alone without entering a room (records are not saved). Trying the rules before playing with friends is a good idea.
