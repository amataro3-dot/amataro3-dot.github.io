Draw X cm!

Party-Arcade mini-gameSense

Draw a line of the target length with your finger. No ruler allowed. It tests how accurate the “1cm” in your head is.

Play Party-Arcade

What kind of game?

The target is between 1cm and 6cm in 0.5cm steps (11 options), the same for everyone. Trace the screen with your finger to draw a line, then tap “OK”. You can redraw as many times as you like within 10 seconds.

The length is measured as the straight distance between the start point and the end point of the line. Curves along the way don’t count.

How to play

  1. During the countdown, check the target length and the “1cm example”.
  2. Trace the screen with your finger to draw a line.
  3. If the length looks wrong, draw it again.
  4. When it looks right, tap “OK”!

Results and ranking

The result shows how far off you were and whether it was too long or too short, like “Off by +0.35cm (too long)”. The smaller the difference, the higher you rank. On the result screen, a bar compares your length with the target. If you don’t draw a line, you’re disqualified (0 points).

Tips to get better

💡 The best tip is to look carefully at the “1cm example” shown during the countdown.
💡 For longer targets, imagine stacking 1cm pieces in your head; it helps you stay accurate.
💡 A straight line makes it easier to judge the length by eye.

Behind the game

🛠 1cm in the game is 1cm on the screen (a length set by the browser). On some phones it may differ slightly from 1cm on a real ruler. That’s exactly why tuning your sense with the example matters.

Practicing alone

From “Practice mode” on the Party-Arcade home screen, you can practice just this game alone without entering a room (records are not saved). Trying the rules before playing with friends is a good idea.
