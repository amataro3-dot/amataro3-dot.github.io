Make a South Indian Curry!

Party-Arcade mini-gameMemory

Memorize the order of the spices going into the pot and make the same curry. A memory game — get both the spices and the order right for a high score!

Play Party-Arcade

What kind of game?

Spices go into the pot one at a time (0.7 seconds each). Each spice is shown large, with a picture of its little dish and its name. There are 6 to 8 spices, and about half the time the same spice goes in twice.

Once you’ve memorized them, you have 10 seconds to answer. Tap spices from the 12 on the shelf in the order they went in to fill the slots. “↶ Undo” lets you redo. When all slots are filled, tap “Done!”. The number of spices and the order are the same for everyone.

How to play

  1. Memorize the spices going into the pot, in order.
  2. Tap spices on the shelf in the order they went in.
  3. When all slots are filled, tap “Done!”.

Results and ranking

The result shows your score and judgments, like “12 pts (◎5 ○2)”. Higher is better. For equal scores, the player who tapped “Done!” earlier ranks higher. 0 points gives 0 pts. On the result screen, the correct recipe and your answer are shown side by side with ◎, ○ and ×.

Scoring

JudgmentPoints
◎ Right spice in the right place2
○ In the recipe, but in the wrong place1
× Not in the recipe (or an empty slot)0

The maximum is the number of spices × 2 (16 points for 8).

The spices (12 kinds)

Mustard seed, cumin, curry leaf, red chili, turmeric, coriander, fenugreek, hing (asafoetida), cardamom, cinnamon, clove, black pepper

Tips to get better

💡 Remembering the color and shape of the pictures is faster than remembering names (yellow powder is turmeric, green leaves are curry leaves, and so on).
💡 Group them in threes and remember them with a rhythm; you’ll forget less.
💡 The same spice may go in twice. If you think “I saw that already”, it’s the second time.
💡 Even if you’re unsure of the order, a spice that was in the recipe still gets ○ for 1 point. Filling a slot is better than leaving it empty.

Behind the game

🛠 The scoring works like the number game “Hit & Blow”. Even if you can’t remember everything, you can still earn points bit by bit.
🛠 Spices are shown on light-colored dishes so that dark spices are easy to see too.

Trivia: South Indian curry and spices

South Indian cooking often uses “tempering”, where spices like mustard seeds and curry leaves are fried in oil first to bring out their aroma. In real cooking, the order of spices matters, but the order in this game is random every time, so it isn’t a real recipe.

Practicing alone

From “Practice mode” on the Party-Arcade home screen, you can practice just this game alone without entering a room (records are not saved). Trying the rules before playing with friends is a good idea.
