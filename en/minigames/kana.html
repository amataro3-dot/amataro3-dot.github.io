Write in Japanese!

Party-Arcade mini-gameNeatness

A chance to try writing Japanese! Write the model hiragana or katakana character with your finger, neatly, in a cross-lined square like a Japanese school notebook. It’s scored out of 100 on 6 points such as shape, size and stroke order.

Play Party-Arcade

What kind of game?

The character is one of 15 hiragana and 15 katakana (the same for everyone). The model is at the top, with a large square to write in below it. Even players who don’t read Japanese write the same Japanese character — just copy the model.

Write one stroke at a time with your finger. You can redo with “Undo stroke” or “Clear”, and tap “OK” when you’re done. The time limit is 15 seconds; if time runs out, what you’ve written so far is scored.

How to play

  1. Look carefully at the model at the top.
  2. Write it stroke by stroke, carefully, in the middle of the square.
  3. If you don’t like it, redo it with “Undo stroke” or “Clear”.
  4. When you’re done, tap “OK”!

Results and ranking

The result is a score out of 100; higher is better. Writing nothing gives 0 points. On the result screen, the model is shown at the top, with everyone’s characters below in order of score.

The 6 scoring items

ItemPointsWhat is checked
Shape40How well your lines overlap the model (compared after matching size and position)
Size15Is it about the same size as the model?
Position15Is it in the middle of the square?
Strokes10Is the number of strokes right? (half if off by one)
Order10The stroke order and the direction each stroke starts in
Smooth10Are the lines steady, not shaky?

If the shape is very different (another character or a scribble), the other items go down too. Scoring is strict: careful writing scores around 80–90.

The characters

Hiragana: あ い う く こ し つ て の へ も ろ る ね め
Katakana: ア イ ウ エ カ キ ク コ サ ス ト ノ ミ ム ロ

Tips to get better

💡 Follow the stroke order. Both the order and the direction you start each stroke (top to bottom, left to right) earn points.
💡 Use the cross lines in the square as a guide, and write in the middle at about the same size as the model.
💡 Rushing makes your lines shaky and lowers “Smooth”. You have 15 seconds, so stay calm.
💡 For square characters like “ロ” and “コ”, make sure the corners connect properly to score higher.

Behind the game

🛠 Each model character is stored as line data, stroke by stroke, and the computer compares it with your lines to score them.
🛠 So that scribbles and other characters can’t score points, a low shape score also lowers the other items.

Hiragana and katakana

Japanese uses two sets of phonetic characters: hiragana (rounded) and katakana (angular). Stroke order is taught in Japanese schools, and following it is what makes characters look neat.

Practicing alone

From “Practice mode” on the Party-Arcade home screen, you can practice just this game alone without entering a room (records are not saved). Trying the rules before playing with friends is a good idea.
