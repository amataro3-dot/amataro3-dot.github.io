Catch the Ruler!

Party-Arcade mini-gameReflexes

Tap quickly to catch a ruler that drops without warning. It’s the classic “ruler drop test” from science class, made playable on your phone.

Play Party-Arcade

What kind of game?

A vertical ruler (0–40cm) is on the screen, and fingers are waiting at the 0cm mark. You never know when the ruler will drop. The moment you think it’s falling, tap anywhere on the screen to catch it.

You get 3 tries, and the ranking is decided by the average of where you caught it (how many cm the ruler fell). The wait before it drops is random each time, between 1.5 and 4 seconds. Sometimes the ruler only twitches as a fake. The waits and fakes are the same for everyone in the room.

How to play

  1. Watch the ruler and wait.
  2. As soon as it starts to fall, tap the screen!
  3. Where you caught it is shown as “N cm!”, and the next try begins.
  4. After 3 tries, the average length is your record.

Results and ranking

The result is shown like “Avg 22.9cm”; shorter is better. A “false start” (tapping before it drops) and a try where it falls without being caught count as 40cm. If all 3 tries fail, you get 0 points. On the result screen, marks on a picture of the ruler show where you caught it each time.

Tips to get better

💡 Watch the ruler itself, not the fingers, and you’ll notice the moment it moves.
💡 A fake only twitches; it doesn’t fall. Tapping in a panic is a false start, so tap after you see it fall.
💡 You can tap anywhere on the screen. Keep your finger wherever is easiest to press.

Behind the game

🛠 This used to be a reflex game where you tapped when red turned green. Now it’s a falling ruler.
🛠 Gravity in the game is about 60% of the real thing, so the ruler falls a little slower than a real one. Even so, it reaches the 40cm end in about 0.37 seconds.

Trivia: reaction time and falling distance

The distance an object falls is “½ × gravity × time × time”. With a real ruler, a 0.2-second reaction lets it fall about 20cm. In this game, it falls about 12cm in 0.2 sec, about 19cm in 0.25 sec and about 27cm in 0.3 sec. The length you caught tells you how fast you reacted.

Practicing alone

From “Practice mode” on the Party-Arcade home screen, you can practice just this game alone without entering a room (records are not saved). Trying the rules before playing with friends is a good idea.
