Flag Up, Flag Down!

Party-Arcade mini-gameReflexes

“Red up!” “White down… NOT!” — it’s the flag game, a Japanese party classic. The calls get faster and faster, with “… NOT!” tricks mixed in.

Play Party-Arcade

What kind of game?

There are two buttons at the bottom of the screen, “RED” and “WHITE”. While you hold a button, that flag is up; let go and it goes down. You can press both at once with two fingers.

The calls appear at the top letter by letter and get faster and faster. The calls are worth 15 points in total. A single call like “Red up!” is 1 point, and a red-and-white call like “Red up… NOT, white down!” is 2 points (red and white are judged separately). The calls are the same for everyone.

The game is played in English, Japanese or Korean, but the calls appear at the same speed in every language, so it’s fair even when players use different languages.

How to play

  1. Read (listen to) the call to the very end before moving a flag.
  2. “up” / “down”: put the flag in that position.
  3. “… NOT!”: don’t move that flag.
  4. Moving a flag that isn’t called is also wrong.

Results and ranking

The result is a score; higher is better. Each time a call is judged, ○ or × appears above it. Even if you make a mistake, the game keeps going.

Scoring

WhatPoints
Each correct call (one part)1 (up to 15)
4 correct in a row+1
8 correct in a row+2
12 correct in a row+3
15 in a row (perfect)+4

A mistake resets the streak to 0. The maximum is 15 + 10 = 25 points. 0 points gives 0 pts.

Tips to get better

💡 The basic rule: wait until the last word before moving. Lots of people fall for “Red up… NOT!”.
💡 With “… NOT!”, moving that flag even for a moment is wrong. If in doubt, don’t move.
💡 If a flag is already up and the call is “up”, leave it. Pressing it again makes it wrong.
💡 The streak bonus is big, so focus on not breaking your streak rather than on one mistake.

Behind the game

🛠 About 30% of the calls are “… NOT!” tricks, and they’re made to appear in the direction you’re most tempted to move.
🛠 So that doing nothing doesn’t earn too many points, most calls can only be answered correctly by moving a flag.
🛠 At first there were 20 parts and a maximum of 35 points; now there are 15 parts and a maximum of 25.

Practicing alone

From “Practice mode” on the Party-Arcade home screen, you can practice just this game alone without entering a room (records are not saved). Trying the rules before playing with friends is a good idea.
