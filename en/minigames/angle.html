Draw the Angle!

Party-Arcade mini-gameSense

Draw a line at the target angle without a protractor. Aim to make the angle between your line and the gray line exactly the target.

Play Party-Arcade

What kind of game?

In the middle of the screen there is a dot and a gray reference line. Drag from the center dot to draw a line; it’s judged by the angle between your line and the gray line.

The target is between 10° and 165° in 5° steps, the same for everyone. The direction of the reference line also changes every time. After you let go, tap “OK!” to confirm. The time limit is 15 seconds.

How to play

  1. Check the target angle and the direction of the gray line.
  2. Drag from the center dot to draw a line.
  3. If it looks good, tap “OK!”. If not, you can draw it again.

Results and ranking

The result shows how far off you were, like “Off by 3.5°”. Smaller is better. On the result screen, you can compare everyone’s lines.

Tips to get better

💡 Use 90° (a right angle) and 180° (a straight line) as landmarks. 45° is half a right angle, 30° is a third, and 135° is a right angle plus 45°.
💡 Thinking of the hands of a clock also helps. One hour is 30°.
💡 When the gray line is slanted, rotate the screen in your head and imagine the line is horizontal.

Behind the game

🛠 The reference line points in a different direction every time, so you can’t just memorize “always draw it in the same place”. Every round is a test of your real sense of angles.

Practicing alone

From “Practice mode” on the Party-Arcade home screen, you can practice just this game alone without entering a room (records are not saved). Trying the rules before playing with friends is a good idea.
