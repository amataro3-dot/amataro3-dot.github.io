Grill My Hamburg Steak!

Party-Arcade mini-gameJudgment

Cook the 3 hamburg steaks in the frying pan to the target doneness. Timing is everything — when to flip, and when to move them to the plate.

Play Party-Arcade

What kind of game?

The steaks appear one after another and start cooking. They appear at different times and cook at different speeds — and the speed changes partway. More sizzle particles means it’s cooking faster.

A steak’s color is the color of “the side cooking underneath right now”. Tap once to flip it; the raw side goes down, so the color goes back to raw. Tap again to move it to the plate. The time limit is 15 seconds. How they cook is the same for everyone.

How to play

  1. Check the target doneness (Rare, Medium rare, Medium or Well-done).
  2. When the bottom side reaches the target color, tap to flip it.
  3. When the other side also reaches the target color, tap again to move it to the plate.
  4. Do the same for all 3. Don’t burn them!

Results and ranking

The result is shown as the average difference from the target doneness, like “Avg error 4.2 (Medium)”. Smaller is better. If all 3 are burnt, you get 0 points.

Scoring

Doneness is shown as a number from 0 to 100: 0 is raw, 100 is burnt.

TargetDoneness
Rare30
Medium rare45
Medium60
Well-done80
  • For each of the 6 sides (both sides of 3 steaks), the difference from the target number is calculated.
  • The average of those 6 differences is your “Result”.
  • A burnt steak, or one not moved to the plate in time, counts as a difference of 100 on both sides.

Tips to get better

💡 Watch the number of sizzle particles. More particles means it’s cooking fast, so flip it early.
💡 The 3 steaks cook at different speeds. Watch each one and finish them one by one without rushing.
💡 Once flipped, you can’t see the color of the side you cooked first. Remember things like “that side was a bit overdone, so take this one off early”.

Behind the game

🛠 The cooking speed changes once per side, without warning (it speeds up about 60% of the time and slows down about 40%). The only clue is the sizzle.

About real hamburg steaks

You can choose “Rare” in the game, but a real hamburg steak is made from ground meat, so cook it all the way through before eating.

Practicing alone

From “Practice mode” on the Party-Arcade home screen, you can practice just this game alone without entering a room (records are not saved). Trying the rules before playing with friends is a good idea.
