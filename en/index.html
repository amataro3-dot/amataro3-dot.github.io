Drawing Telephone

2–8 playersDrawing

Pass a word along using only drawings. Somehow the cat turns into a pig…? Check the answer together at the end.

Read more

Pass a secret word along to the next player using only drawings. You can only see the previous drawing for 10 seconds, so the word slowly changes. The best part is the end, when everyone’s drawings are replayed and you find out where it all went wrong.

Great for: Laughing together, no matter how well you draw. Choose the words from different categories. The worse the drawings, the funnier the answer check at the end.

  3. 💬STEP 3Share the number by message or out loud
  4. 🎉STEP 4When everyone is in, start!

📲 Add the site to your home screen to open it like an app (Android: Chrome menu → “Add to Home screen” / iPhone: Safari Share → “Add to Home Screen”).

About Muddy's Games

Muddy's Games is a handmade game site that started with a simple wish: “I want games we can all play together when friends get together.”

Every game here runs in your phone’s browser. There is no app to install and no account to create. Create a room, share the 4-digit number, and friends right next to you or far away can join the same game in seconds.

What matters most is having fun as a big group. Party-Arcade lets up to 8 people play at once, and the ranking changes with every mini-game. In Drawing Telephone, the more players there are, the more the drawings drift, and everyone ends up laughing together at the end.

More games will be added little by little.
