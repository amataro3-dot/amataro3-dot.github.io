How to Play Hit & Blow

A number-guessing game where you find your opponent’s secret number using hints. Whoever guesses first wins. It’s a mix of deduction and a little luck.

Play Hit & Blow

Players

Online, up to 8 people can join one room. Two players at a time can battle on each of the 4 floors, A–D, all at once. Anyone without a seat can watch any floor. There is also “Practice alone”, where you guess the computer’s number by yourself.

Switch the language on the first screen (日本語 / English / 한국어). Each player sees the game in their own language, so friends using different languages can play in the same room.

Getting started

  1. Enter your name and tap “Create a room”.
  2. Tell everyone the 4-digit room code on the screen.
  3. Everyone enters the code and taps “Join”.
  4. Tap a seat on floor A–D to sit down (2 per floor).
  5. The person who created the room (the host) chooses 3 or 4 digits and taps “Start”. Every floor with 2 players starts at the same time.

Choosing your secret number

  • Choose a 3-digit (or 4-digit) number using the digits 0–9.
  • You can’t use the same digit twice. A number like “112” isn’t allowed.
  • Numbers starting with “0” (for example, “012”) are fine.
  • If you can’t decide, tap “Random” to pick one at random.
  • Tap a box at the top to put a digit into that box. You can, for example, fill in the middle one first.

Hit and Blow

On your turn, “call” the number you think your opponent has. You get a hint back.

  • Hit (H): the number of digits that are right and in the right place
  • Blow (B): the number of digits that are right but in the wrong place

Example: when the opponent’s number is “472”

CallHintWhy
1731H 0B7 is in the same place
2470H 3BAll three digits are in it, but all in different places
4782H 0B4 and 7 are in the same places
4723H 0BCorrect!

Winning and losing

  • Who goes first is chosen at random and announced at the start. Players take turns calling.
  • The first player to guess the opponent’s number exactly (all hits) wins.
  • If the first player guesses it, it’s “Sudden Death”: the second player gets one more chance. If the second player also guesses it, it’s a draw.
  • After the match, you can see each other’s secret numbers.
  • If your opponent leaves and doesn’t come back within 10 seconds, you win by forfeit.
  • When all floors are finished, the host taps “Play again” to return to the lobby.

Memo

Your memo is visible only to you. Your opponent can’t see it.

  • Number memo: each tap on a 0–9 button changes it: “✕ (not used)” → “○ (maybe used)” → “none”.
  • Digits marked ✕ are dimmed on the number buttons above and in your call history (the buttons can still be pressed). Digits marked ○ are circled in your history.
  • Position memo: tap a □ above the memo, then a number, to note “this place might be this digit”. Tap a filled □ again to clear it.

Watching

People without a seat, and players whose match is already over, can switch floors with the A–D buttons at the bottom of the screen and watch. You can see both players’ calls and hints, and the number an opponent is typing. Secret numbers stay hidden until the match ends.

Tips for winning

💡 Start with calls using fresh digits, like “012” and “345”, to quickly narrow down which digits are in the number.
💡 “0H 0B” is a big chance: none of those digits are used. Mark them ✕ in your memo.
💡 You can type your next call during your opponent’s turn. “Call” becomes available when it’s your turn.
💡 In Practice alone, your best number of tries is saved.
