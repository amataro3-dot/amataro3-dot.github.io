How to Play CUT & CLAIM

A territory game where you draw lines across a rectangular field and claim the areas you enclose. Up to 8 players fight over the same field at once.

Play CUT & CLAIM

Players

Online, up to 8 people can join one room. If you don’t have enough players, the host can add computer players (CPUs) and choose their level: Easy, Normal or Hard. There is also “Practice”, where you play against CPUs by yourself.

Getting started

  1. Tap “Online battle”, enter your name and tap “Create a room”.
  2. Tell everyone the 4-digit room code shown on the screen.
  3. Everyone else enters the code and taps “Join”.
  4. If you need more players, the host taps “Add CPU” (2–8 players in total, people and CPUs).
  5. The host taps “Start” to begin.

Controls

  • You are the △. It always moves forward on its own. Usually it runs along the “lines” (the outer frame and the edges of claimed land).
  • On a line, press the arrow that points toward open space to head into the field. You leave your own line behind you.
  • Inside, use the arrows to turn (you can’t turn straight back).
  • Hold the A button to dash (double speed). The ring around A is your gauge. It only refills while you are on a line.

Claiming land

  • Head inside and get back to the outer frame or your own land to close the shape.
  • Of the areas you split, the side without Diayan (below) becomes your land. If neither side has Diayan, you get the smaller one.
  • You can draw lines through other players’ land too. Enclose it, and it becomes yours.

Two enemies

  • Diayan: a long, thin white ◇ that spins and flies around the field. If it touches a line you are drawing in open space, you are out (lines inside other players’ land are safe).
  • Astan: a lime ＊ that runs along the lines. Touch it and you are out. When you go inside, it chases you along your line.

When you are out

  • Another player touches the line you are drawing (the one drawing the line is out, not the one who touched it)
  • You hit your own line
  • Two players crash head-on inside (both are out)
  • Diayan or Astan touches you
  • You are on a line inside an area someone encloses

When you are out, the line you were drawing disappears, but your land stays. You come back after 5 seconds.

Winning

The first player whose land passes the target wins. The target depends on the number of players.

PlayersTarget
2–350%
4–535%
6–830%

If nobody reaches the target in 3 minutes, the player with the most land wins. After the match, the host can tap “Back to lobby (everyone)” to bring everyone back.

Tips

💡 Small shapes and quick returns are safest. The longer you stay inside, the easier you are to catch.
💡 When Astan is close, don’t go inside. Wait for it to pass.
💡 Save some gauge so you can dash on the way back.
💡 If another player’s line is nearby, it’s your chance to touch it and take them out.
