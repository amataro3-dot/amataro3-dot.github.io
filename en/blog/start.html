How Muddy's Games began

October 30, 2026

A game that everyone can enjoy together at parties and get-togethers. That simple wish is where Muddy's Games began.

A paper maze, on a phone

The first game was “Labyrinth .VS”. It is based on an invisible-wall maze game that used to be played on paper or as a board game. On paper, though, getting ready takes a lot of work — you have to draw your maze so your opponent can’t see it, and so on. “It would be great to play this right away on our phones.” That thought was the starting point.

Made together with AI

The games are made by talking things through with AI. At first they were made with Gemini, but fixing one thing would break something else. Fix after fix, it never ended… So partway through, the switch was made to Claude, and now the rules are worked out together while building.

Playing it at a party

  • Even people who aren’t playing lean in to watch the screen and share the suspense
  • When an opponent bumps into a wall placed somewhere sneaky, the whole room goes wild
  • Spectators betting coins on who will win makes it even more fun

Wanting more of that fun, more games were added — Party-Arcade, Drawing Telephone and Hit & Blow — all games that “everyone there can play right away, with no app”. More will keep coming, little by little.
