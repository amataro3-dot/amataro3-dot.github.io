How the Party-Arcade mini-games were born

October 30, 2026

A love of party games

It all comes from a love of party games where you play one mini-game after another. Party-Arcade makes that playable with nothing but your friends’ phones. Up to 8 people compete for points in a different mini-game each time.

A restaurant menu turned into games

Some of the mini-games are based on the menu of the creator’s own restaurant. “Build a Muddy’s Burger!”, “Grill My Hamburg Steak!”, “Make a South Indian Curry!”, “Pour the Jack!” — dishes and drinks made at the restaurant every day became games you can make on your phone.

The burger gets the biggest reaction

The game that gets the biggest reaction is “Build a Muddy’s Burger!”. The result screen shows the burger you stacked, crooked shape and all, so showing them off to each other is great fun. And then there’s the ketchup at the end! The way it comes out of the wiggly bottle is the best part.

Swapping in better games

At first there were games like “Tap Battle”, “Reflex Test” and “Late Rock-Paper-Scissors”. But the goal was to make better games of the same type, so they were replaced little by little. For example, “Reflex Test” became “Catch the Ruler!”, where you catch a falling ruler, and “Late Rock-Paper-Scissors” became “Flag Up, Flag Down!”.

There are 15 games now. The search for even more fun games will go on, playing them at parties along the way.
