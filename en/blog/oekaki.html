Why Drawing Telephone was built from scratch

October 30, 2026

The app everyone played was gone

Before, a similar drawing telephone app was the go-to. Then one day that app disappeared. “Then why not make one?” — and that is how Drawing Telephone was born.

It’s a game where a word is passed along to the next player using only drawings, and the last player guesses the answer. You can only see the previous drawing for 10 seconds, so the word slowly changes along the way.

The hardest part: sending drawings

The hardest part to build was delivering each drawing to everyone’s phones. Unlike words or numbers, a drawing is a collection of many lines. Getting it to the next player’s phone in exactly the same shape took a lot of effort.

The favorite feature: replay in drawing order

The feature with the most care put into it is the replay at the end, where everyone’s drawings are animated in the order they were drawn. You don’t just see the finished picture — you see things like “Wait, they started drawing from there!?”

Playing it at a party

  • At the final reveal, the answer is nothing like the original word
  • During the replay, everyone is glued to the screen
  • Hunting for the culprit: “Where did the word change?”
  • And the one drawing by someone who is truly bad at drawing

You don’t need to be good at drawing — in fact, the worse the drawings, the more fun it gets. That’s the kind of game it is.
