How to Play Drawing Telephone

Pass a topic along using only drawings, and the last player guesses what it was. At the end, everyone watches how the drawings changed — and laughs.

Play Drawing Telephone

Players

2 to 8 players. The more people play, the more the drawings drift — and the more fun it gets.

Switch the language on the first screen (日本語 / English / 한국어). The topic is shown to each player in their own language, so friends using different languages can play in the same room.

Getting started

  1. Enter your name and tap “Create a room”.
  2. Tell your friends the 4-digit room code on the screen.
  3. Friends enter the code and tap “Join”.
  4. The first person in the room (the host) chooses a topic genre and taps “Start game”.

How a game goes

The order is chosen at random at the start.

  1. Player 1: look at the topic and draw it in 30 seconds.
  2. Player 2: look at player 1’s drawing for just 10 seconds. When it disappears, draw it from memory in 30 seconds (you can’t see the topic).
  3. From player 3 on: the same — look at the previous drawing, remember it, draw it.
  4. Last player: look at the previous drawing and answer what the topic was, in 30 seconds.

Tap “Done” when you finish drawing to pass it on, even if there’s time left.

How to draw

  • Trace with your finger to draw lines.
  • There are 6 colors: black, red, blue, green, yellow and white (works as an eraser). Switch line width with “Thin” and “Thick”.
  • “Undo” removes the last line. “Clear” lets you start over.

Checking the answer

  • You can answer in English, Japanese or Korean — any of them counts.
  • For English, capitals, “a / an / the”, plurals, spaces and hyphens don’t matter.
  • Some topics accept other ways of saying them too (for example “bike” for bicycle).
  • If you disagree with the result, the host can switch “Mark as correct / Mark as wrong” on the results screen.

Results

The topic, the last player’s answer and everyone’s drawings are shown in order. Tap “Replay from the start” to watch each drawing animated in the order it was drawn. Try to find where the topic went off track!

Tips for fun

💡 Big, easy-to-spot features get across better than small details.
💡 Remembering a drawing in 10 seconds is surprisingly hard! Remember the colors and numbers of things, not just the shapes.
💡 It’s fine if it doesn’t get across. Watching the drawings change is the best part of this game.
