How to Play Party-Arcade

A party game where you battle in a different mini-game each round, and the highest total score becomes champion.

Play Party-Arcade

Players

Up to 8 people can play at the same time in one room. There is also a “Practice mode” you can play alone.

Getting started

  1. Enter your name and create a room. The person who creates it becomes the host.
  2. Tell your friends the 4-digit room ID so they can join.
  3. The host chooses a mode in the lobby and starts the game.

The screen language can be switched on the first screen (日本語 / English / 한국어). Each player sees the game in their own language, so friends using different languages can play in the same room.

Modes

Arcade

For 4 to 8 rounds, a different mini-game is chosen at random each time. No game repeats until every game has appeared once, so you get to compete in many kinds of games. The player with the highest total wins. Arcade needs 2 or more players (alone, try Single Game or Practice mode).

  • Double Up: points are doubled in one round, announced right before that game.
  • Final Up: points are doubled in the final round, so a comeback is possible until the very end.

Single Game

Choose one mini-game and everyone plays it once. No points are given. Great for a quick match in a game you are curious about.

Practice mode

Practice mini-games alone without entering a room. Records are not saved.

Players with the same result share the same rank. If you fail or don’t finish in time, you get 0 points. To see which result decides the ranking in each game, check the “Mini-game list” and “Scoring details” below.

👑 Title holder

For each game, the player with the best record ever becomes the “Title holder” — just one person on the whole site. Their name and record appear on the countdown screen before the game starts.

  • Only records from Arcade with 2 or more players in the room count. Records from Single Game and Practice mode don’t count.
  • Failed results (0 points) don’t count. If two records are the same, the player who set it first keeps the title.
  • When you beat the record, the result screen shows “👑 New record! Title won!”.
  • In Make a South Indian Curry!, the maximum score depends on the number of spices, so records are compared as a percentage of the maximum.

Scoring details

Flag Up, Flag Down!

  • The calls are worth 15 points in total. A single call like “Red up!” is 1 point, and a red-and-white call like “Red up… NOT, white down!” is 2 points (red and white are judged separately).
  • “up” / “down”: correct if the flag is in that position at the moment of judging. If it’s already in that position, moving it even for a moment is wrong.
  • “… NOT!”: moving that flag even for a moment is wrong.
  • Moving a flag that isn’t called also makes that part wrong.
  • Streak bonus: 4 in a row +1, 8 in a row +2, 12 in a row +3, 15 in a row (perfect) +4. A mistake resets the streak to 0.
  • The maximum is 15 + 10 = 25 points. 0 points gives 0 pts. Players with the same score share the same rank.

  • For each of the 6 sides (both sides of 3 steaks), the difference from the target number is calculated.
  • The average of those 6 differences is your “Result”. Smaller is better.
  • A burnt steak, or one not moved to the plate in time, counts as a difference of 100 on both sides.
  • If all 3 are burnt, you get 0 pts.

Example: if the target is Medium (60) and you cook the first steak to 58 on one side and 63 on the other, the differences are 2 and 3.

Red Light, Green Light!

  • Players who touch the watcher (reach the goal) always rank above those who don’t. Among goal players, a faster time ranks higher.
  • Players who don’t reach the goal (caught or out of time) rank higher the shorter the “m to go”. The watcher is 25m away.
  • If you don’t move at all from the start, you get 0 pts.
  • DASH: while held, you speed up and reach the top speed of 3.5m/s (about 13km/h) in about 1.2 sec.
  • If you press nothing, you slow down gradually (from top speed you stop after about 1.7 sec and about 3m).
  • BRAKE: you can stop from top speed in about 0.3 sec and about 0.5m. If you press both, the brake wins.
  • You are safe for 0.2 sec after the watcher turns around. After that, any movement means you’re out.
  • The watcher says the call 3 or 4 times (the same for everyone). The last call crosses the 15-second mark; after finishing it and turning around, the watcher keeps watching for a moment and the game ends.

There are 6 to 8 spices, so the maximum is the number of spices × 2 (16 points for 8). When the same spice goes in twice, you score for both if you get both. For equal scores, the player who tapped “Done!” earlier ranks higher. 0 points gives 0 pts.

Example: if the recipe is “Cumin → Curry leaf → Turmeric” and your answer is “Curry leaf → Cumin → Turmeric”, you get ○ ○ ◎ for 4 points.

Tips for fun

💡 Reading the rules during the countdown is the shortcut to winning. Try new games in Practice mode first.
💡 Turn on Double Up or Final Up and the ranking keeps changing until the end.
💡 If a new game doesn’t show up, close the browser tab once and open it again. Old data may be left over.
💡 Even if your connection drops for a moment, reopen the page and you’ll be back in the same room.
